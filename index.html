The Newsletter

Ernie's Weekly Column
Vol. XXIII · Week 1 · April 2026

Ryan Opens Title Defense With a Statement; Greg Opens His Season With Whatever the Opposite of a Statement Is

Listen, I've been covering this league since Bush was president—the first time, not the second time, and certainly not whatever we're doing now—and I can tell you that Week 1 never means anything. Except when it does. And this week? It might mean something.

Ryan rolled into the 2026 season the way Ryan rolls into every season: with a plan, a spreadsheet, and the quiet confidence of a man who has won this thing before and knows he'll win it again. An 8-4 demolition of Greg isn't surprising. What's surprising is that Greg managed to win four categories. NSB, ERA, and NSVH—the three categories that most clearly say "my hitters aren't doing anything so at least my bullpen showed up."

"Twenty-three seasons in, and Greg still drafts like a man who believes in miracles. Admirable, really. Misguided, but admirable."

Meanwhile, Drew handled Jeremy 7-5 in a matchup that was closer than it should have been. Drew's pitching was characteristically stingy—the man hoards Ks and QS like a dragon sitting on a pile of gold—but Jeremy's power numbers kept it interesting. Five categories from a team most people had penciled in for the bottom third? Jeremy might actually be dangerous this year. Or it might be Week 1 noise. Ernie has seen this movie before.

The real story of the week might be Jake's 7-4 win over Brian. Jake's been steadily climbing the Elo rankings for three years now, and an opening-week win over a veteran like Brian—complete with dominant hitting across four categories—suggests the climb isn't done yet. Brian, for his part, looked like a man still figuring out his roster. Four categories won on vibes and relievers. Classic early-season Brian.

And then there's Cory. An 8-3 win over David is the kind of opening statement that makes people pay attention. Cory's always had the tools—he drafts well, he works the wire—but he's never quite put a full season together. This could be the year. Or it could be the same thing we say every April. Either way, David's going to need more than three categories a week to survive in this league.
